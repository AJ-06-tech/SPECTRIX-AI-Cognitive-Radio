3. CONCLUSION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
ASSM achieved higher packet delivery ratio and throughput in all four tested scenarios.
It also substantially reduced collisions across the tested synthetic scenarios.
Latency improvement was not consistent across all user counts.
These results demonstrate the behavior of the proposed algorithm under the simulated conditions. They do not establish performance in a real wireless deployment.
